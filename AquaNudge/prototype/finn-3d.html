Happy

Water 70–100%

Fins fanned wide, rich colour, smiling eyes. Darts about, blows bubbles and stops to look at you.

Restless

Water 40–70%

Half-lidded eyes and slightly lower fins. Paces back and forth along the glass.

Worried

Water 15–40%

Eyes turned up to the waterline under a tilted lid, fins held in. Hangs near the top and gulps.

Parched

Water under 15%

Faded colour, heavy eyelids, limp fins. Rests on the sand with gills pumping and the odd weak flop.
